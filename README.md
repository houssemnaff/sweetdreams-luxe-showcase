# Sweet Dreams Delight

Act as an expert UI/UX designer and full-stack web developer. Build a modern, highly graphical, fully responsive, and visually stunning landing page and product catalog for a high-end luxury patisserie named "Sweet Dreams".

I. Core Brand Identity & Palette (Extremely Important):

Override any dark themes and strictly apply the exact brand color palette extracted from the "Sweet Dreams" logo:

- Background Color (Primary): Pure White (#FFFFFF) to keep the aesthetic clean, bright, and modern.

- Primary Accents (Gold): Use a luxurious metallic gold scheme blending Dark Gold (#C68A2C) for typography/borders and Light Gold (#FCE688) for highlights and gradients.

- Secondary Accents (Pastel Pink): Use Soft Pastel Pink (#F9BACD) and Light Highlight Pink (#FDE2EA) for subtle card backgrounds, tag badges, and decorative accents.

- Typography: Brand Name "Sweet Dreams" must be styled in an elegant, high-end cursive luxury script font in Dark Gold (#C68A2C).

II. Hero Section & Background:

- High-graphic intensity and ultra-sleek layout on a crisp #FFFFFF background.

- Integrate a seamless, looping background video or overlay showcasing delicious high-end cakes and patisserie crafting.

- Overlay text with high contrast, elegant typography, and a metallic gold CTA button ("Explore Catalog").

III. Navigation & Product Categories:

Create dedicated, beautifully animated product cards without displaying prices. Each card must feature high-resolution image placeholders, descriptions, and an "Order via WhatsApp" micro-interaction:

1. Gâteaux d'Anniversaire (Birthday Cakes):

   - "Choco-Luxe Birthday Cake" - Rich chocolate layers with gold leaf details

   - "Red Velvet Dream" - Cream cheese frosting and fresh berries

2. Gâteaux de Fiançailles (Engagement Cakes):

   - "Royal White Elegance" - Multi-tier floral engagement cake

   - "Golden Rose Tier" - Custom fondant with edible gold accents

3. Salés & Traiteur (Savory & Traditional Pastries):

   - "Assorted Mini Pizza & Pâté Box" - Box of savory bites

   - "Authentic Kaak Warka" - Traditional Tunisian almond pastry (500g)

   - "Traditional Baklawa Tray" - Premium pistachio and almond layers

IV. Social Media & Direct Integrations:

- Instagram Link: Prominent, stylish icon linking directly to: https://www.instagram.com/amal_sghairi/?hl=fr with smooth gold hover effects.

- Direct WhatsApp Ordering: Sticky/floating button and inline product order buttons linked directly to WhatsApp number +21624220772. Pre-fill the WhatsApp chat message with the contact name displayed as "Sweet Dreams".

V. Interactive Features:

- Micro-interactions on hover for product cards (subtle gold shadow #C68A2C, pink badge pulse #F9BACD).

- Sticky responsive navigation bar with clean white (#FFFFFF) background and dark gold links.

- 100% mobile-optimized layout.
